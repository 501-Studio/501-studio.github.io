## Current product programs

### Project DURA
An early-stage biomedical R&D program exploring a next-generation dural substitute concept. The program is focused on preserving barrier function after surgery while enabling controlled tissue integration over time.

Public product brief: https://501lab.com/products/dura.html

Current public status:
- clinical problem definition completed
- neurosurgical specialist feedback incorporated into product requirements
- design architecture defined
- engineering and biocompatibility validation planned

Project DURA is research-stage technology and is not presented as an approved or clinically validated medical device.

### Project MIND
A digital mental wellbeing product under development, focused on low-friction mood check-ins, routine continuity, and safety-aware support pathways.

Public product brief: https://501lab.com/products/mind.html

Project MIND is being developed as a wellbeing product. It is not presented as a diagnostic or treatment service.
