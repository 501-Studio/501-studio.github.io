Interactive prototype

Daily check-in.Prototype

A small browser prototype exploring Project MIND's core interaction: notice how the day is going, keep a few protective routines visible, and reduce the effort required to check in.

01 · Mood

How does today feel?

Choose the closest answer. There is no score or diagnosis.

02 · Routine anchors

Keep the basics visible.

This prototype uses a few generic examples. A future product would let users define their own.

03 · Optional note

Anything worth remembering?

Keep it short. This demo stores information only in this browser on this device.

Prototype and safety note. This is an early product prototype, not a clinical tool. It does not diagnose, prevent, or treat depression or any other condition. It is not intended for crisis support. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis service in your region.
