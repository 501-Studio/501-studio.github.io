How we work

Evidence first. Safety aware. Built to iterate.

Biomedical concepts and digital products require different validation paths. We keep exploratory work clearly separated from validated outcomes and build each program around explicit next-step evidence.

01

Problem before product

We define the clinical or user problem, constraints, and measurable development targets before expanding the solution.

02

Research-grade documentation

Literature, experimental planning, technical decisions, and product assumptions are documented so they can be reviewed and revised.

03

Rapid digital iteration

Software is used to shorten feedback cycles, prototype internal workflows, and move product ideas toward testable implementations.

Claude integration roadmap

AI workflows we plan to implement.

These are planned Claude API integrations, not currently deployed AI product features. Draft outputs will be traceable and reviewed by a human before publication or research use.

01 · Project DURA · Planned

Literature-to-evidence workflow

Turn uploaded scientific papers into a source-linked evidence table covering materials, methods, and outcomes, then draft test checklists and internal research briefs for researcher approval.

02 · Project MIND · Planned

Wellbeing content generation

Draft brief, non-diagnostic mood-check-in questions, routine prompts, and opt-in weekly reflections from user-approved inputs. Build safety boundaries and review procedures before release.

03 · Internal operations · Planned

Documentation automation

Convert structured test notes and engineering change records into reviewable experiment summaries, issue updates, and software release notes, reducing repeated manual writing.
